# Career Leadership References

Curated references for IC career growth, technical writing, leadership, interviews, stakeholder communication, AI-era career development, and post-mortems.

## IC Career Growth

1. **Staff Engineer: Leadership beyond the management track** -- Will Larson (2021). Definitive guide to staff+ roles, archetypes, and operating at scale.
2. **The Staff Engineer's Path** -- Tanya Reilly (2022). Practical guide covering big-picture thinking, project execution, and leveling up.
3. **StaffEng.com** -- https://staffeng.com -- Collection of staff engineer stories and guides curated by Will Larson.
4. **levels.fyi Engineering Levels** -- https://www.levels.fyi/blog/swe-level-framework.html -- Cross-company comparison of engineering levels and compensation.
5. **Dropbox Engineering Career Framework** -- https://dropbox.github.io/dbx-career-framework/ -- Public IC ladder from IC1 through IC6 (Principal).
6. **CircleCI Engineering Competency Matrix** -- https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/131XZCEb8LoXqy79WWrhCX4sBnGhCM1nAIz4feFZJsEo -- Detailed skills matrix across levels.
7. **Rent the Runway Engineering Ladder** -- https://dresscode.renttherunway.com/blog/ladder -- One of the first publicly shared IC ladders.
8. **progression.fyi** -- https://progression.fyi -- Directory of public engineering career frameworks from dozens of companies.
9. **Brag Documents** -- Julia Evans. https://jvns.ca/blog/brag-documents/ -- How to maintain a running record of accomplishments for promotions.
10. **Get your work recognized: write a brag document** -- Julia Evans (2019). Template and methodology for self-advocacy.
11. **On Being A Senior Engineer** -- John Allspaw. https://www.kitchensoap.com/2012/10/25/on-being-a-senior-engineer/ -- Classic essay on mature engineering behavior.
12. **The Engineer/Manager Pendulum** -- Charity Majors. https://charity.wtf/2017/05/11/the-engineer-manager-pendulum/ -- Career path flexibility between IC and management.
13. **Thriving on the Technical Leadership Path** -- Keavy McMinn (2023). Perspectives from a principal engineer at GitHub.
14. **Being Glue** -- Tanya Reilly (2019). Talk on the invisible work that holds teams together. https://noidea.dog/glue
15. **How to Get Promoted** -- Lenny Rachitsky newsletter. Frameworks for understanding promotion mechanics at big tech companies.

22. **Writing Technical Design Docs** -- Angela Zhang (2020). Practical guide to design docs with examples.
23. **The Anatomy of a 6-Page Memo** -- Jesse Freeman. Breakdown of Amazon's narrative-driven document culture.
24. **Mailchimp Content Style Guide** -- https://styleguide.mailchimp.com -- Model for clear, accessible technical writing voice.
25. **GitLab Handbook: Writing Style Guide** -- https://about.gitlab.com/handbook/communication/ -- Async-first writing standards.
26. **Shape Up** -- Basecamp. https://basecamp.com/shapeup -- Pitches and bets as async communication artifacts.
27. **Asynchronous Communication: Definitive Guide** -- Doist blog. Patterns for remote-first teams.
28. **Technical Writing Courses** -- Google. https://developers.google.com/tech-writing -- Free courses on technical writing fundamentals.
29. **Diátaxis Framework** -- https://diataxis.fr -- Four-quadrant approach to documentation (tutorial, how-to, reference, explanation).
30. **Every Page is Page One** -- Mark Baker (2013). Topic-based technical writing methodology.

## Leadership & Management

31. **An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management** -- Will Larson (2019). Systems thinking applied to engineering organizations.
32. **The Manager's Path** -- Camille Fournier (2017). Career guide from IC through CTO, with focus on tech lead and manager transitions.
33. **Radical Candor** -- Kim Scott (2017). Framework for caring personally while challenging directly.
34. **Situational Leadership II** -- Ken Blanchard. Adapting leadership style (directing, coaching, supporting, delegating) to team readiness.
35. **The GROW Model** -- John Whitmore. Coaching framework: Goal, Reality, Options, Will.
36. **Turn the Ship Around!** -- L. David Marquet (2013). Intent-based leadership and pushing authority to information.
37. **Team Topologies** -- Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais (2019). Organizing teams for fast flow (stream-aligned, enabling, platform, complicated-subsystem).
38. **Drive** -- Daniel Pink (2009). Intrinsic motivation through autonomy, mastery, and purpose.
39. **The Five Dysfunctions of a Team** -- Patrick Lencioni (2002). Trust, conflict, commitment, accountability, results pyramid.
40. **High Output Management** -- Andy Grove (1983). Classic on leverage, meetings, and output-oriented management.
41. **Resilient Management** -- Lara Hogan (2019). Practical guide to building trust, growing teams, and navigating change.
42. **Engineering Management for the Rest of Us** -- Sarah Drasner (2022). Approachable guide to leading engineering teams.
43. **The Art of Leadership: Small Things, Done Well** -- Michael Lopp (2020). Essays on leadership moments that compound.
44. **Debugging Teams** -- Brian Fitzpatrick & Ben Collins-Sussman (2015). Collaboration patterns for software teams (HRT: Humility, Respect, Trust).
45. **Accelerate** -- Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim (2018). Research-backed metrics for engineering team performance (DORA).

51. **Never Split the Difference** -- Chris Voss (2016). FBI negotiation tactics applicable to salary and offer discussions.
52. **Cracking the Coding Interview** -- Gayle Laakmann McDowell (2015). Standard reference for algorithm interviews.
53. **interviewing.io** -- https://interviewing.io -- Practice interviews and data on interview performance.
54. **Blind** -- https://www.teamblind.com -- Anonymous professional network for compensation data and interview experiences.
55. **The Pragmatic Engineer Newsletter: Hiring** -- Gergely Orosz. Inside look at how hiring works at top companies.
