# Career Strategy in the AI Era (2026)

**Reality check:** entry-level engineering hiring has collapsed (~73% down). Routine, well-defined coding is the first thing automated. But senior and staff roles survive and grow because they rest on work AI cannot own.

## What AI Can't Do (Why Senior+ Survives)

- Architecture decisions with real business tradeoffs.
- Business context: knowing WHAT to build and why it matters.
- Verification judgment: knowing when output is actually correct.
- Stakeholder alignment and cross-team negotiation.
- Team leadership and mentorship.

## Skills That APPRECIATE

- System design
- Security thinking
- Code review judgment
- Communication (written and verbal)
- Debugging complex, distributed systems
- Product thinking (connecting code to user/business outcomes)

## Skills That DEPRECIATE

- Syntax memorization
- Boilerplate writing
- Mechanical refactoring
- First-draft documentation

## Strategy: Become the Orchestrator

The durable role is the person who:
1. Decomposes an ambiguous problem into workable pieces.
2. Delegates the mechanical work to AI.
3. Verifies the output rigorously.
4. Owns the result end-to-end.

## Where to Invest

- **Judgment speed:** review AI output fast and catch what's wrong.
- **Context loading:** understand the business and domain deeply so you direct AI well.
- **Architectural thinking:** design the systems AI then implements.

## Proof Points

Frame your value around leverage and outcomes, not craft:
- Better: "I ship 3x more by orchestrating AI and owning the result."
- Weaker: "I write elegant code by hand."

The market pays for owned outcomes, not for typing. Position yourself as the engineer who turns ambiguity into shipped, verified systems.
