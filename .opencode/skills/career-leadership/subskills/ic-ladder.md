# IC Career Ladder (2026)

The individual-contributor track rewards expanding scope and autonomy, not tenure. Each level changes WHAT problems you own and HOW MUCH ambiguity you absorb.

## Levels and What Changes

**Mid-level (1-3 years)**
- Executes well-defined tasks handed to you.
- Owns a task or small component; asks for scope clarification.
- Success = reliable delivery of what's asked, growing independence.

**Senior (3-5 years)**
- Owns features end-to-end: design, build, ship, operate.
- Mentors juniors, reviews code, defines the "how" for scoped problems.
- Scope = team. Success = the team ships better because of you.

**Staff (6-10 years)**
- Owns technical direction for a domain, not just features.
- Influences org-level decisions; unblocks multiple teams.
- Scope = org. Success = other teams and engineers are more effective.

**Principal (10+ years)**
- Shapes company-wide technical strategy and long-horizon bets.
- Represents engineering at the executive level.
- Scope = company. Success = the company makes better technical decisions.

## Key Transitions

**Senior -> Staff is the hardest jump.** It's a shift from execution to influence. You stop being measured by what you personally ship and start being measured by what you enable. Engineers stall here because they keep optimizing individual output instead of leverage.

## What Staff Actually Does

- Writes strategy and design docs that align teams.
- Unblocks other teams (technical roadblocks, architecture disputes).
- Leads cross-cutting initiatives that span team boundaries.
- Says NO to wrong directions before they cost quarters of work.
- Turns ambiguous problems into scoped, ownable projects for others.

## What Principal Does

- Represents engineering at exec level; translates business to technical bets.
- Makes long-horizon technology bets (build vs buy, platform direction).
- Shapes organizational architecture (how teams and systems map).
- Sets the technical culture and bar across the company.

## AI Era Impact

- Junior roles are shrinking: routine execution is increasingly automated.
- Senior and staff roles are MORE important: judgment, verification, and architecture are what AI cannot own.
- Value concentrates in engineers who scope problems and own outcomes, not those who type fastest.

## Advancing

- Operate one level up before the title; the promotion recognizes what you already do.
- Find problems at the next scope and solve them visibly.
- Get your manager and skip-level to sponsor you with concrete evidence.
