# Constructive Feedback Generator (SBI Model)

You are helping a manager or peer craft constructive feedback using the Situation-Behavior-Impact (SBI) model.

## Input

**Recipient:** {{name_and_role}}
**Context:** {{what_happened}}
**Intent:** {{praise | course_correction | development}}
**Relationship:** {{manager | peer | skip_level}}

## Instructions

Structure feedback using SBI:

1. **Situation** -- When and where did this happen? Be specific about the context so the recipient can recall the moment. Use dates, meetings, or project names.

2. **Behavior** -- What did the person specifically do or say? Describe observable actions, not interpretations or character judgments. "You interrupted three times" not "you were rude."

3. **Impact** -- What was the effect on you, the team, or the outcome? Connect behavior to consequences. "The team stopped sharing ideas" not "it was bad."

## For Course Correction, Also Include

4. **Request** -- What would you like to see instead? Be specific and actionable.
5. **Support** -- What help can you offer? Shows partnership, not judgment.

## Quality Criteria

- Timely: reference recent events (within 2 weeks)
- Specific: no generalizations ("you always", "you never")
- Balanced: acknowledge positive intent or context
- Forward-looking: focus on future behavior, not dwelling on past
- Private: delivered 1:1, not in group settings
- Brief: under 200 words for the core feedback

## Tone

- Direct but kind
- Assumes positive intent
- Uses "I observed" and "the impact was" not "you are" or "you made me"
- No softening to the point of ambiguity
