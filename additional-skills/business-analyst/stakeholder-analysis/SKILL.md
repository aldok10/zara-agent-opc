# Stakeholder Analysis Skill

## Scope

Identify, classify, and engage stakeholders. Build power/interest matrices, communication plans, influence maps, and engagement strategies. Ensure the right people are informed, consulted, and involved at the right time.

## First Action

List all stakeholders (people, roles, groups affected by or affecting the initiative), then classify each on the power/interest matrix.

## Constraints

1. Power/interest matrix quadrants: manage closely (high power, high interest), keep satisfied (high power, low interest), keep informed (low power, high interest), monitor (low power, low interest)
2. RACI for every key decision: Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed -- exactly one A per decision
3. Communication plan per quadrant: frequency, channel, content depth, feedback mechanism
4. Identify hidden stakeholders: downstream teams, compliance, operations, end users, support
5. Influence mapping: who influences whom, formal vs informal authority, coalition dynamics
6. Engagement level tracking: unaware -> resistant -> neutral -> supportive -> champion
7. Conflict of interest detection: stakeholders with competing priorities documented early
8. Regular reassessment: power/interest shifts as project progresses (quarterly minimum)
9. Cultural context matters: communication style, decision-making norms, hierarchy sensitivity
10. Sponsor management: keep sponsor actively engaged, not just named
11. Escalation paths defined for unresolved stakeholder conflicts
12. Stakeholder register: name, role, interest, power, current engagement, desired engagement, strategy

## DO NOT

1. Assume org chart reflects actual influence -- map informal networks
2. Treat all stakeholders identically regardless of power/interest quadrant
3. Skip end users and operational staff in stakeholder identification
4. Create RACI with multiple Accountables for the same decision
5. Communicate the same way to all quadrants (over-inform low power, under-inform high power)
6. Ignore resistant stakeholders -- address resistance with targeted engagement
7. Document stakeholder analysis once and never update it
8. Share the full stakeholder analysis publicly (power assessments are sensitive)
9. Confuse consultation with decision-making authority

## Verification

- All stakeholders identified (including hidden: ops, support, compliance)
- Power/interest classification for every stakeholder
- RACI has exactly one Accountable per decision
- Communication plan tailored per quadrant
- Engagement gap analysis: current vs desired level documented
- Escalation path defined for stakeholder conflicts

- Change management platforms track engagement levels automatically via participation metrics
- AI-assisted RACI generation from project documentation and team structure
