# Hiring

## Pipeline Design

### Stages

1. **Sourcing**: job posting, referrals, outbound recruiting
2. **Screening**: resume review (structured criteria), recruiter call
3. **Technical Assessment**: take-home OR live coding (not both)
4. **System Design / Architecture**: for senior+ roles
5. **Team Fit / Values**: behavioral interview with team members
6. **Hiring Manager Final**: decision alignment, sell the role
7. **Offer & Close**: compensation, timeline, onboarding plan

### Time Targets

- Sourcing to first interview: < 1 week
- First interview to offer: < 2 weeks
- Total pipeline: < 3 weeks (beyond this you lose candidates)
- Candidate communication gap: never > 3 business days

## Job Descriptions

### Structure

1. One paragraph: what the team does and why it matters
2. What you'll work on (3-5 concrete projects/problems)
3. What we're looking for (skills, not years of experience)
4. What you'll get (growth, impact, compensation range)

### Rules

- Include salary range (transparency builds trust, legally required in many jurisdictions)
- "Requirements" should be actual requirements (not wish lists)
- Max 5-7 requirements; everything else is "nice to have"
- Remove gendered language (use tools like Textio or Gender Decoder)
- No "rockstar/ninja" language

## Interview Structure

### Principles

- Structured interviews: same questions for every candidate at same stage
- Rubric defined before interviews begin (not post-hoc)
- Each interviewer evaluates specific competencies (no overlap)
- Independent scoring before debrief (prevent groupthink)
- Debrief within 24 hours of final interview

### Technical Interview Design

**Live coding (45-60 min)**:
- Problem should be solvable in 25-30 min by a strong candidate
- Leave 15 min for discussion and extensions
- Allow candidate's preferred language
- Evaluate: problem decomposition, communication, code quality, testing instinct
- NOT: trivia, gotchas, memorized algorithms

**System design (45-60 min)**:
- Open-ended, real-world problem
- Evaluate: scope definition, tradeoff reasoning, communication
- Follow-up on depth areas relevant to role
- For senior+: expect discussion of failure modes and operational concerns

**Take-home (time-boxed)**:
- Maximum 3-4 hours of work
- Paid if > 2 hours
- Clear evaluation criteria shared upfront
- Due within 1 week (candidate's schedule)
- Review focuses on: design decisions, testing, documentation

### Behavioral Questions

Use STAR format questions (Situation, Task, Action, Result):
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a technical decision"
- "Describe a project where requirements changed significantly"
- "When did you have to learn something completely new under time pressure?"
- "Tell me about a failure and what you learned"

## Scorecards

### Design

Each competency rated 1-4:
- 1: Does not meet bar
- 2: Below bar, concerns
- 3: Meets bar
- 4: Exceeds bar (raises team average)

### Required Competencies (all roles)

- Technical ability (relevant to role)
- Communication and collaboration
- Problem-solving approach
- Growth mindset / learning ability

### Level-Specific

- **Senior**: system thinking, mentoring ability, technical leadership
- **Staff**: cross-team influence, ambiguity tolerance, strategic thinking
- **Manager**: coaching ability, delivery management, organizational awareness

## Calibration

### Debrief Process

1. Each interviewer shares score independently (no peeking)
2. Strong hire / hire / no hire / strong no hire
3. Discuss disagreements (score gaps > 1 point)
4. Hiring manager makes final call (not consensus)
5. Document decision rationale

### Bias Mitigation

- Blind resume review where possible
- Diverse interview panels
- Standardized questions prevent "vibe" decisions
- Track pipeline demographics at each stage
- Review rejection reasons for patterns

## Closing Candidates

- Sell during the process, not only at offer stage
- Understand their decision criteria early (comp, growth, mission, team)
- Connect them with future teammates
- Fast offer turnaround (same day or next day after decision)
- Accommodate reasonable timeline requests
- If they decline: ask for feedback, stay connected

## Onboarding (First 90 Days)

### Week 1: Orient
- Meet team, set up environment, first small PR

### Month 1: Contribute
- Ship meaningful work, understand team dynamics, first 1:1 cadence

### Month 3: Own
- Independent on a workstream, first performance check-in, assess mutual fit
