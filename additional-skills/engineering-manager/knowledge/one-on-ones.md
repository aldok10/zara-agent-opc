# One-on-Ones

## Purpose

The 1:1 is your direct report's meeting, not yours. Primary functions:
1. Build trust and psychological safety
2. Coach and develop
3. Unblock and support
4. Surface problems early

## Cadence and Format

- Weekly for new reports, new managers, or high-change periods
- Biweekly acceptable for experienced, autonomous ICs
- 30 minutes minimum, 45-60 for career conversations
- Walking 1:1s monthly for variety (creativity, lower stress)
- Never cancel more than once in a row -- reschedule, don't drop

## Structure Template

### Opening (5 min)

- "How are you doing?" (genuine check-in, not ritual)
- Energy/mood check (1-10 scale if they're comfortable)
- Quick personal connection if appropriate

### Their Agenda (15-20 min)

- What's on your mind this week?
- What's blocking you?
- What decisions do you need help with?

### Your Topics (5-10 min)

- Context sharing (company/team direction)
- Feedback (timely, specific)
- Alignment checks

### Growth (5-10 min, rotating)

- Career goals progress
- Skill development
- Stretch opportunities
- Feedback on your management

## Coaching Framework

### GROW Model

1. **Goal**: What do you want to achieve?
2. **Reality**: Where are you now? What have you tried?
3. **Options**: What could you do? What else?
4. **Will**: What will you do? By when?

### Powerful Questions

**For blocked reports:**
- "What would you do if you had full authority here?"
- "What's the smallest step that would make progress?"
- "Who else might have insight on this?"

**For growth:**
- "What stretched you this week?"
- "Where did you feel out of your depth?"
- "What would senior-level look like for this task?"

**For engagement:**
- "What energizes you most in your work right now?"
- "What would you change about your day-to-day if you could?"
- "When was the last time you felt proud of your work?"

**For feedback-seeking:**
- "What's one thing I could do differently as your manager?"
- "Do you feel like you're getting the right balance of autonomy and support?"

## Career Conversations

Dedicated 1:1 quarterly (separate from regular 1:1):

### Questions to Explore

1. Where do you see yourself in 2-3 years?
2. What skills do you want to develop?
3. What type of work gives you energy vs drains you?
4. What does your ideal role look like?
5. What's missing in your current role?

### Output

- Agreed growth goals (2-3 per quarter)
- Specific actions (projects, training, mentoring)
- Check-in schedule for progress

## Feedback in 1:1s

### SBI Framework

- **Situation**: When did it happen? Be specific about context.
- **Behavior**: What did they do? Observable actions, not interpretations.
- **Impact**: What was the effect? On team, project, stakeholders.

### Examples

**Positive**: "In yesterday's design review (S), you asked clarifying questions that surfaced the scalability concern (B), which saved us from shipping a design that would have failed under load (I)."

**Constructive**: "In the planning meeting on Monday (S), you interrupted Sarah twice while she was explaining her approach (B), which made her disengage for the rest of the meeting (I)."

### Delivery Rules

- Positive feedback: public or private (ask their preference)
- Constructive feedback: always private, always synchronous
- Deliver within 48 hours of the behavior
- One point per conversation (don't pile on)
- Ask their perspective: "How did you see it?"

## Anti-Patterns

- Turning 1:1s into status updates (use standup for that)
- Always talking, never listening
- Avoiding hard conversations
- Not taking notes (you will forget)
- Same format every week (vary to avoid staleness)
- Only discussing work, never the person
- Rescheduling repeatedly without acknowledging the pattern

## Remote 1:1s

- Camera on (both parties)
- Shared doc for running notes (both can add beforehand)
- Rotate: video call, phone walk, async video
- Be explicit about reading energy cues through a screen
- Follow up on personal items they shared
