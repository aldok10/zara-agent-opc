# One-on-Ones

Structure, coaching techniques, career conversations, and feedback delivery for engineering managers.

## When to Load

- Preparing for or improving 1:1 meetings
- Having difficult conversations with reports
- Coaching engineers on career growth
- Delivering constructive feedback effectively

## Key Concepts

**Structure** -- Their meeting, not yours. 10/10/10 format: 10 min their topics, 10 min your topics, 10 min growth/career. Or: mood check, blockers, development, action items. Adapt per person.

**Coaching vs Directing** -- Ask before telling. "What have you tried?" before "Here is what to do." Coach for growth (they find the answer). Direct only for urgent/safety situations.

**Career Conversations** -- Separate from performance discussions. Focus: where do they want to be in 1-2 years? What skills gap exists? What opportunities can you create? Revisit quarterly.

**Feedback Delivery** -- SBI model: Situation (when/where), Behavior (what you observed), Impact (effect on team/project). Timely (within 48h), specific (not "you need to communicate better"), actionable.

**Psychological Safety** -- They share problems early only if past sharing was met with help, not blame. Build trust by: following through on commitments, admitting your mistakes, protecting them from above.

## Patterns

```markdown
# 1:1 Prep Template

## Their Updates
- (Let them fill this in advance or start the conversation)

## Check-in
- Energy level this week?
- Anything blocking you?
- How is the current project going?

## My Topics
- [Specific feedback or context to share]
- [Upcoming changes that affect them]

## Growth
- Progress on [development goal]?
- Any stretch opportunities coming up?

## Action Items (from last time)
- [ ] [Previous commitment - status?]
```

```markdown
# SBI Feedback Example

Situation: "In yesterday's design review..."
Behavior: "...you interrupted Alex three times while she was presenting her approach."
Impact: "She stopped contributing for the rest of the meeting, and we missed her perspective on the caching layer."

Follow-up: "I want to make sure everyone has space to contribute. What do you think happened there?"
```

```markdown
# Career Conversation Framework

1. Where are you now? (Current role satisfaction, strengths, challenges)
2. Where do you want to go? (1-2 year vision, not just next promotion)
3. What is the gap? (Skills, experience, visibility needed)
4. What is the plan? (Specific actions, timeline, support needed from me)
5. How will we track? (Check-in cadence, milestones, adjustment triggers)
```

## Gotchas

- Canceling 1:1s repeatedly signals "you are not important" -- reschedule, never cancel
- Using 1:1s for status updates wastes the relationship-building opportunity
- Giving only positive feedback trains people to brace for "the but" when you finally give critical feedback
- Career conversations without follow-through action destroy trust faster than not having them
- Coaching questions that are actually thinly veiled directives ("Don't you think you should...?")

## Verification

- Meeting happens weekly at a consistent time (not constantly rescheduled)
- Reports bring topics (indicates they see it as their meeting, not a status report to you)
- Feedback is delivered within 48h of the event (not stockpiled for review season)
- Career goals have concrete next steps with dates (not vague aspirations)
- Action items from previous 1:1 are reviewed (commitments are tracked)
