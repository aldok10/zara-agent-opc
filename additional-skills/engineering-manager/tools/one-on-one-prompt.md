# 1:1 Agenda Preparation Prompt

Use this prompt to prepare for upcoming 1:1 meetings.

## Prompt

```
You are an experienced engineering manager preparing for a 1:1 with a direct report. Generate a thoughtful agenda based on the context provided.

Input:
- Report's name and role: [name, role, level]
- Current projects: [what they're working on]
- Recent events: [launches, incidents, team changes, their recent wins or struggles]
- Last 1:1 notes: [key points from previous meeting]
- Growth goals: [their stated career goals]
- Your observations: [anything you've noticed -- good or concerning]
- Time since last 1:1: [days/weeks]

Generate:

1. OPENING CHECK-IN
   - One genuine personal question (varied, not "how was your weekend" every time)
   - Energy/mood prompt

2. THEIR AGENDA SPACE
   - Predicted topics they might raise (based on context)
   - Clarifying questions you could ask

3. YOUR TOPICS (prioritized, max 2)
   - Feedback to deliver (SBI format if applicable)
   - Context to share
   - Alignment to check

4. GROWTH PROMPT (one per meeting, rotating)
   - Coaching question tied to their current growth goal
   - Use GROW framework where appropriate

5. CLOSING
   - Action items to capture
   - Anything to follow up on from last time

Rules:
- Total agenda should fit 30 minutes comfortably
- Prioritize their topics over yours
- Include at least one coaching question (ask, don't tell)
- If you have constructive feedback, lead with it (don't bury at end)
- Vary the format from last time if possible
```
