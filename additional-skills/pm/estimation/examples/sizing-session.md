# Example: Planning Poker Session

## Setup

Team: 4 developers, 1 facilitator. Anchor story: "Add filter to list page" = 3 points.

## Story 1: "User password reset via email"

- Round 1 votes: 3, 3, 5, 3
- Outlier (5) explains: "Email service integration is untested"
- Team: "We've used it before, template exists"
- Round 2: 3, 3, 3, 3
- **Result: 3**

## Story 2: "Dashboard with 4 charts"

- Round 1 votes: 8, 5, 8, 13
- Discussion: "13 because charting library is new" vs "5 because charts are simple"
- Clarification: team has used chart library once before
- Round 2: 8, 8, 8, 8
- **Result: 8**

## Story 3: "Migrate payment provider"

- Round 1 votes: 13, 21, ?, 21
- "?" person: "I don't understand the rollback plan"
- After discussion: still high uncertainty
- **Result: 21 + flag for spike/decomposition**

## Story 4: "Add dark mode toggle"

- Round 1 votes: 2, 3, 2, 2
- Close enough, no re-vote needed
- **Result: 2**

## Story 5: "GDPR data export endpoint"

- Round 1 votes: 5, 8, 5, 5
- Outlier (8): "Need to handle nested relations"
- Team agrees it's slightly harder than anchor
- **Result: 5** (majority, within 1 step)

## Session Duration: 18 minutes for 5 stories
