# Example: Company OKR Set with Cascade

## Company OKRs (Q3)

**O1: Accelerate product-led growth**
- KR1: Increase self-serve signups from 3K to 6K/mo (aspirational)
- KR2: Improve trial-to-paid conversion from 8% to 13% (aspirational)
- KR3: Achieve $200K new MRR from self-serve (committed)

**O2: Deliver enterprise-grade reliability**
- KR1: Achieve 99.95% uptime (committed)
- KR2: Reduce P1 mean-time-to-resolve from 4h to 30min (aspirational)
- KR3: Pass SOC2 Type II audit (committed)

**O3: Build a world-class team**
- KR1: Reduce time-to-productivity from 30d to 14d (aspirational)
- KR2: Maintain eNPS above 50 (committed)
- KR3: Fill 8 critical roles with <45d avg time-to-hire (committed)

---

## Team Cascade: Product Team (ladders to O1)

**O1: Make first-run experience effortless**
- KR1: Reduce time-to-first-value from 12min to 4min
- KR2: Increase Day-7 activation rate from 28% to 42%
- KR3: Reduce onboarding drop-off from 60% to 30%

## Team Cascade: Platform Team (ladders to O2)

**O1: Build infrastructure for five-nines reliability**
- KR1: Reduce unplanned downtime from 3.2h/mo to <25min/mo
- KR2: Achieve <200ms P99 latency under 5x current load
- KR3: Implement automated failover for all critical services (committed)

## Alignment Map

```
Company O1 <- Product O1 (activation), Growth O1 (acquisition)
Company O2 <- Platform O1 (reliability), Security O1 (compliance)
Company O3 <- People O1 (hiring), Engineering O1 (onboarding)
```
