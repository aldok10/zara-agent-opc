# Scoring Rubric

## Score Levels with Examples

### 0.0 - No meaningful progress

| KR | Outcome |
|----|---------|
| Increase NPS from 32 to 45 | NPS stayed at 32 (or dropped) |
| Reduce P1 incidents from 8/mo to 2/mo | Still at 8/mo |
| Achieve 500 enterprise trials | Got 12 trials |

### 0.3 - Minimal progress, well below target

| KR | Outcome |
|----|---------|
| Increase NPS from 32 to 45 | NPS moved to 36 |
| Reduce P1 incidents from 8/mo to 2/mo | Down to 6/mo |
| Achieve 500 enterprise trials | Got 150 trials |

### 0.5 - Halfway, meaningful but insufficient

| KR | Outcome |
|----|---------|
| Increase NPS from 32 to 45 | NPS at 38 |
| Reduce P1 incidents from 8/mo to 2/mo | Down to 5/mo |
| Achieve 500 enterprise trials | Got 250 trials |

### 0.7 - Strong result, aspirational target zone

| KR | Outcome |
|----|---------|
| Increase NPS from 32 to 45 | NPS at 41 |
| Reduce P1 incidents from 8/mo to 2/mo | Down to 3/mo |
| Achieve 500 enterprise trials | Got 380 trials |

### 1.0 - Fully achieved (or exceeded)

| KR | Outcome |
|----|---------|
| Increase NPS from 32 to 45 | NPS at 45+ |
| Reduce P1 incidents from 8/mo to 2/mo | Down to 2 or fewer |
| Achieve 500 enterprise trials | Got 500+ trials |

## Scoring Decision Tree

1. Calculate: (Actual - Baseline) / (Target - Baseline)
2. Cap at 1.0, floor at 0.0
3. For non-numeric KRs: map qualitative milestones to 0.0/0.3/0.5/0.7/1.0
4. Document data source used for scoring
5. Flag if measurement method changed mid-quarter

## Red Flags in Scoring

- All KRs at 1.0 -> goals too easy, raise the bar
- All KRs at 0.3 -> goals disconnected from reality, or execution failure
- Wide variance (0.0 and 1.0 mix) -> priority/focus problem
- Scores with no data attached -> reject, rescore with evidence

## 2024-2025 Updates

### Google Grading Colors (Aspirational OKRs)

| Score | Color | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 0.7-1.0 | Green | On track |
| 0.4-0.6 | Yellow | At risk |
| 0.0-0.3 | Red | Off track |

### Committed vs Aspirational Scoring (Reclaro 2025)

- **Committed KRs**: binary - must hit 100%. Score: 1.0 or 0.0. No partial credit.
- **Aspirational KRs**: 70% = green (strong). 100% = you sandbagged.
- Perfect existing scoring process > adopting new frameworks prematurely
- Focus: consistency of measurement, not sophistication of rubric

### Weekly Confidence Rating

Format for weekly check-ins:

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| On Track (>70% confidence) | Progressing as expected | Continue |
| At Risk (40-70% confidence) | Obstacles emerging | Discuss blockers, adjust tactics |
| Off Track (<40% confidence) | Unlikely to hit target | Escalate, reprioritize, or reset KR |

Update weekly. Two consecutive "At Risk" → requires intervention plan.

### Scoring AI-Adoption KRs

- Measure outcome (time saved, quality delta), not tool adoption
- Baseline must be pre-AI measurement (don't compare to zero)
- Account for ramp-up: first 4 weeks may show regression before gains
- Score on steady-state performance (weeks 5+ post-adoption)
