# User Interviews

## The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick) — Three Rules

1. Talk about their life instead of your idea
2. Ask about specifics in the past instead of generics or opinions about the future
3. Talk less and listen more

**Bad data signals**: compliments, fluff, hypotheticals ("I would...")

## Commitment & Advancement (Rob Fitzpatrick)

Meetings succeed when they end with commitment. Three currencies of commitment:
- **Time**: follow-up meeting, trial period, testing your prototype
- **Reputation**: intro to decision-maker, public endorsement, referral
- **Money**: letter of intent, deposit, pre-order, payment

"Compliments are worthless as data because they cost nothing to give."

Escalation ladder: follow-up → intro to decision-maker → pilot → LOI → payment. If they won't advance, you don't have signal—you have politeness.

## JTBD Question Patterns

- "When did you last [try to accomplish X]?"
- "Walk me through what happened step by step"
- "What were you trying to do? What triggered that?"
- "What did you try first? Then what?"
- "What was hardest about that?"
- "How do you solve this today? What's the workaround?"
- "What would it mean for you if this was solved?"
- "Who else is involved in this decision?"

## Interview Structure

| Phase | Duration | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Warm-up | 3 min | Rapport, context setting |
| Core | 20 min | Behavior stories, JTBD |
| Dig deeper | 10 min | Follow-ups on surprises |
| Wrap-up | 2 min | Anything else, referrals |

## Follow-up Prompts

- "Tell me more about that"
- "Why was that important?"
- "What happened next?"
- "How did that make you feel?"
- "Can you show me?"

## Synthesis

1. Extract key quotes per interview (max 5)
2. Identify opportunity statements: "User needs a way to [X] because [Y]"
3. Affinity map across interviews (group by theme, not person)
4. Count frequency: how many interviews surface same opportunity
5. Rate intensity: language strength, emotional weight
6. Feed into OST as validated/emerging opportunities

## Recruiting

- Recruit for behavior, not demographics ("people who have done X in last 30 days")
- Mix: current users + churned + non-users solving same problem
- Min 5 interviews per opportunity area for pattern detection
- Incentivize appropriately ($50-100 for 30min B2B, gift cards B2C)
- Schedule rolling: 2/week minimum during active discovery

## Anti-patterns

- **Leading questions**: "Don't you think X would be useful?"
- **Confirmation bias**: only hearing what validates your hypothesis
- **Power user only**: interviewing loudest customers, missing silent majority
- **Feature requests as insights**: "Users want button X" vs understanding why
- **No synthesis**: interviews happen, but insights don't feed decisions
- **Hypothetical acceptance**: "Would you use X?" (always yes, meaningless)
