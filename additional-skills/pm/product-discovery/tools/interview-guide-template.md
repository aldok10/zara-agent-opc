# Interview Guide Generator

## Input Required
- Target opportunity area: [what you're exploring]
- User segment: [who you're interviewing]
- Key assumptions to probe: [max 3]

## Output Structure

```
INTERVIEW GUIDE: [Opportunity Area]
Segment: [who] | Duration: 30 min | Date: [date]

WARM-UP (3 min)
- Tell me about your role and what a typical day looks like
- How long have you been doing [relevant activity]?

CORE: BEHAVIOR STORIES (20 min)
- When did you last [activity related to opportunity]?
- Walk me through what happened step by step
- What triggered you to do that?
- What was the hardest part?
- How are you solving this today?
- What have you tried that didn't work?
- [Assumption-specific probe 1]
- [Assumption-specific probe 2]

DIG DEEPER (10 min)
- You mentioned [surprise]  -  tell me more about that
- Why was that important to you?
- Who else is involved in [process]?
- What would it mean if this was solved?

WRAP-UP (2 min)
- Anything else about [topic] I should have asked?
- Know anyone else who deals with this I could talk to?
```

## Rules
- Never mention your solution or product ideas
- Ask about past behavior, never hypotheticals
- Follow energy: when they lean in, dig deeper
- Silence is a tool - pause 3 seconds before filling gaps
