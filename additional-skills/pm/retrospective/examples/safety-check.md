# Example: Safety Check with Low Score

## Context
- Team of 7, sprint 18. Recent: missed deadline, tense stakeholder meeting.
- Facilitator: Dana (rotated, not team lead)

## Safety Check
- Tool: Slido anonymous poll, 1-5 scale
- Results: 2, 3, 2, 4, 3, 2, 3 → **Average: 2.7**

## Facilitator Response

> "Our safety score is 2.7 — below 3. That tells me not everyone feels safe sharing openly today. That's OK and important information. Let's address this before we try a regular retro."

## Pivot: Trust-Building (25min)

1. **Anonymous concerns** (5min): "Write one thing affecting your comfort — anonymous sticky, I'll read themes only."
   - Themes surfaced: blame culture in last post-mortem, fear of speaking up to lead
2. **Norms review** (10min): revisited team agreements, added "critique process not people"
3. **Appreciation round** (10min): each person names one thing they value about another team member

## Adjusted Retro (30min remaining)
- Ran shortened Start/Stop/Continue focusing only on process
- Used anonymous stickies for all input (not named)
- Formed 2 action items (norm-related, not technical)

## Actions
1. WHAT: Rewrite post-mortem template to remove "who" section | WHO: Dana | WHEN: next week | DONE: template updated
2. WHAT: Team lead opens next retro with own mistake/learning | WHO: James | WHEN: sprint 19 | DONE: shared at retro start

## Follow-up
- Safety score sprint 19: 3.6 (improvement tracked)
