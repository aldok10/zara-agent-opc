# Retro Facilitation Techniques

## Core Principles

- Retrospectives generate improvements, not blame
- Every format needs: safety check → gather data → generate insights → decide actions
- Variety prevents retro fatigue (rotate formats)
- Time-box strictly: 60-90 min max

## 4Ls (Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed For)

### Format
| Column | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Liked | What went well? What should we keep doing? |
| Learned | What new knowledge did we gain? |
| Lacked | What was missing? What held us back? |
| Longed For | What do we wish we had? |

### When to Use
- Teams new to retros (low-barrier format)
- After completing a milestone or project
- When you want balanced positive/negative discussion

### Facilitation Tips
- Start with Liked (positive opening)
- Lacked vs Longed For distinction: Lacked = absent, Longed For = desired future
- Combine similar items before discussion
- Timebox: 5 min write, 10 min per column discussion

## Starfish

### Format
Five categories arranged as starfish arms:
```
        Keep Doing
       /          \
More Of            Less Of
       \          /
    Start Doing  Stop Doing
```

### When to Use
- Team needs more nuance than just good/bad
- When "less of" is more actionable than "stop"
- Mid-project check-ins (not just end-of-sprint)

### Facilitation
- Explain difference: "Less of" ≠ "Stop" (reduce, don't eliminate)
- "More of" validates existing good behavior
- "Start doing" surfaces new ideas
- Vote to prioritize: each person gets 3 dots

## Sailboat

### Format
Visual metaphor:
- **Wind** (sails): what propels us forward
- **Anchor**: what holds us back
- **Rocks** (ahead): risks/obstacles we see coming
- **Island** (destination): our goal/where we're heading

### When to Use
- Sprint planning + retro combined
- Forward-looking teams (rocks force proactive thinking)
- When team needs to reconnect with goals
- Visual thinkers and remote teams (miro/figjam friendly)

### Facilitation
- Draw the boat on shared board
- Start with Island (align on destination)
- Then Wind → Anchor → Rocks (end with actionable risks)
- Top-voted rocks become sprint risks to mitigate

## Energy Levels

### Format
Each person rates their energy/morale on a scale (1-5 or traffic light) anonymously, then discuss:
- What's draining energy?
- What's giving energy?
- What one change would improve energy most?

### When to Use
- Team seems burned out or disengaged
- After intense period (deadline, incident, crunch)
- As a quick pulse check (15-20 min standalone)
- When you suspect issues people aren't voicing

### Facilitation
- Anonymous survey first (Mentimeter, Slido, or sticky notes face-down)
- Show aggregate results (not individual)
- Don't force people to explain low ratings
- Focus: "What can WE change?" (not external factors beyond control)

## Remote Retro Adaptations

### Tooling
| Tool | Best For | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Miro/FigJam | Visual formats (sailboat, starfish) | Templates, voting, timer |
| EasyRetro/Retrium | Structured retro flow | Built-in formats, action tracking |
| Notion/Confluence | Async retros | Written format, threaded discussion |
| Metro Retro | Variety of formats | Pre-built templates, anonymity |

### Remote-Specific Challenges
- **Silence**: use silent writing phase (everyone types simultaneously)
- **Domination**: round-robin speaking, use raise-hand feature
- **Distraction**: shorter format (45 min max), camera on
- **Follow-up**: actions tracked in team tool (Linear/Jira), not retro board

### Async Retro Format
For distributed teams across time zones:
```
Day 1: Board opens, silent writing (24h window)
Day 2: Voting on items (24h window)
Day 3: 30-min sync call to discuss top-voted items and agree actions
```

## Facilitation Techniques

### Safety Check (start every retro)
Quick anonymous poll: "How safe do you feel speaking honestly today?" (1-5)
- Average <3: address safety first before proceeding
- Average 3-4: acknowledge, create explicit ground rules
- Average 4+: proceed with full format

### The "1-2-4-All" Pattern
1. Individual reflection (1 min, silent)
2. Pairs discuss (2 min)
3. Groups of 4 share (4 min)
4. All: each group shares top insight

Prevents: groupthink, loudest-voice dominance, cold-start silence

### Dot Voting
- Each person gets 3-5 votes
- Can stack votes on same item (shows strong preference)
- Top 2-3 items become discussion topics
- Time: 2 min voting, prevents endless discussion of everything

### Action Items That Stick
- Maximum 2-3 actions per retro (more = none get done)
- Each action has: owner, due date, definition of done
- First agenda item NEXT retro: "Did we do last retro's actions?"
- No owner = no action (don't assign to "the team")

## Anti-Patterns

- Same format every time (leads to autopilot responses)
- No action items (retro becomes venting session)
- Actions never reviewed (learned helplessness)
- Manager runs retro on their own team (inhibits honesty)
- Blame language ("you did X") vs system language ("X happened because...")
- Retro after every sprint but nothing ever changes (trust erodes)
- Skipping retros "because we're too busy" (when team most needs reflection)
- Only discussing process, never discussing interpersonal dynamics
