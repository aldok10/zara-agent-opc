# Format Library

## Quick Reference

| Format | Structure | Time | Best For | Group Size |
|--------|-----------|------|----------|------------|
| Start/Stop/Continue | 3 columns: start, stop, keep | 45-60min | Quick retros, any team | 3-12 |
| Sailboat | Wind, Anchor, Rocks, Island | 60min | Creative teams, goal focus | 4-10 |
| Timeline | Events plotted chronologically + mood | 75-90min | Post-incident, complex sprints | 4-8 |
| 4Ls | Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed For | 60min | Learning emphasis | 4-12 |
| Mad/Sad/Glad | 3 emotional columns | 45-60min | Emotional check-in, low morale | 3-10 |
| DAKI | Drop, Add, Keep, Improve | 60min | Process improvement focus | 4-10 |
| Starfish | More, Less, Keep, Start, Stop | 60min | Nuanced feedback (5 options) | 5-12 |
| Hot Air Balloon | Fire (lifts), Sandbags (drags), Storm clouds (risks) | 60min | Variation on Sailboat, visual | 4-10 |
| Lean Coffee | Topic-based, voted agenda, time-boxed | 45-60min | Self-organizing teams, varied topics | 3-8 |
| Happiness Radar | Rate happiness per category (process, tech, team) | 30-45min | Quick pulse, trend tracking | 3-15 |
| World Cafe | Rotate small groups through themed tables | 90min | Large teams, cross-team retros | 12-30 |
| Speed Car | Engine (drives), Parachute (slows), Abyss (risks) | 60min | Similar to Sailboat, action-oriented | 4-10 |

| AI-Art Gallery | Team draws/generates AI art representing sprint, discuss (emerging format, limited adoption) | 60min | Creative reset, fun | 4-10 |
| AI Impact Retro | What AI helped, what it broke, what we'd automate next | 60min | AI-heavy teams, tooling eval | 3-12 |
| Data-Driven 5 Whys | Combine 5 Whys with velocity/burndown/cycle time data | 75min | Root cause + evidence | 4-8 |
| Silent Writing Async | Individual write → async vote → sync discuss top 3 only | 45min | Remote-first, async-friendly | 3-20 |

## Selection Guide

- **Default/quick**: Start/Stop/Continue or Happiness Radar
- **Creative/visual**: Sailboat, Hot Air Balloon, or AI-Art Gallery
- **Post-incident**: Timeline (mandatory for SEV1+)
- **Learning-focused**: 4Ls
- **Emotional pulse**: Mad/Sad/Glad
- **Process tuning**: DAKI or Starfish
- **Large group**: World Cafe or Lean Coffee
- **Data-driven**: Happiness Radar, Data-Driven 5 Whys
- **AI/tooling focused**: AI Impact Retro
- **Async/remote-first**: Silent Writing Async
