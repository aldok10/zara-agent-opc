# Facilitation Formats

## Universal Pattern

1. **Silent Write** (5min) — everyone posts individually, no discussion
2. **Group & Reveal** (5min) — cluster similar items, read aloud
3. **Dot Vote** (3min) — each person gets 3-5 votes, pick top 3-5 themes
4. **Discuss** (25-35min) — top-voted items, time-boxed per item
5. **Action Items** (10min) — owner + due + done-definition for top issues
6. **Close** (5min) — read back actions, appreciation round

## Format Catalog

### Start/Stop/Continue
- Structure: 3 columns. What to start, stop, keep doing.
- Timebox: 45-60min total.
- Best for: quick retros, new teams, when energy is low.
- When: default choice, works universally.

### Sailboat
- Structure: Wind (helps), Anchor (slows), Rocks (risks), Island (goals).
- Timebox: 60min.
- Best for: creative teams, goal-oriented reflection, mid-project check-ins.
- When: team needs metaphor to unlock different thinking.

### Timeline
- Structure: Plot events on sprint timeline, mark energy/mood per event.
- Timebox: 75-90min.
- Best for: post-incident, complex sprints with many events, onboarding context.
- When: need shared understanding of what happened and when.

### 4Ls (Liked/Learned/Lacked/Longed For)
- Structure: 4 quadrants capturing each L.
- Timebox: 60min.
- Best for: learning-focused teams, after training/workshops, growth mindset.
- When: emphasize learning over blame.

### Mad/Sad/Glad
- Structure: 3 emotional columns.
- Timebox: 45-60min.
- Best for: emotional check-in, low-energy teams, surfacing unspoken frustrations.
- When: team seems disengaged or frustrated.

## Rotation Schedule

- Track format used per retro in shared doc/tool.
- After 3 consecutive uses of same format: mandatory switch.
- Suggested cycle: S/S/C → Sailboat → 4Ls → Mad/Sad/Glad → Timeline → repeat.
- Facilitator picks based on sprint context + heuristic below.

## Selection Heuristic

| Context | Recommended Format |
|---------|--------------------|
| Normal sprint, no incidents | Start/Stop/Continue |
| Creative/product-heavy sprint | Sailboat |
| Post-incident or complex sprint | Timeline |
| Learning emphasis, after workshops | 4Ls |
| Low morale, emotional undercurrent | Mad/Sad/Glad |
| Team needs energy boost | Any + add energizer opening |

## Energizers (2-5min openers)

- **Rose/Thorn/Bud**: one good, one bad, one upcoming thing (personal or work)
- **Two Truths One Lie**: quick icebreaker for new teams
- **GIF mood**: share a GIF representing your sprint mood
- **One-word sprint**: describe sprint in one word, discuss patterns

## Verification

- Format matches sprint context (not just habit)
- Silent write phase respected (no early discussion)
- Dot voting used to prioritize (not facilitator picking)
- Time stayed within box per section
