# Safety Check

## The Safety Check (run at retro start)

Ask: "On a scale of 1-5, how safe do you feel sharing openly today?"
- 1 = not safe at all
- 3 = somewhat safe
- 5 = completely safe

Collect anonymously (poll tool, anonymous sticky, or hand count with eyes closed).

## Interpreting Results

| Average Score | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| 4.0-5.0 | Proceed normally |
| 3.0-3.9 | Acknowledge, set norms explicitly, proceed with care |
| <3.0 | STOP regular retro  -  run trust-building instead |

## When Safety Is Low (<3.0)

1. Acknowledge: "Safety is low today. Let's address that first."
2. Do NOT ask "why" publicly  -  that requires the safety you don't have
3. Options:
   - Anonymous written concerns (facilitator reads themes only)
   - Smaller breakout groups (2-3 people) for 10min
   - Skip retro, schedule 1:1s, reconvene next week
   - Facilitator shares vulnerability first (model safety)
4. Never force it  -  low safety retro produces garbage data

## Establishing Norms (first retro or after low score)

Propose and agree as team:
- Vegas rule: what's said here stays here
- No blame: discuss systems, not people
- Equal air: everyone speaks, no one dominates
- Assume good intent
- It's OK to pass

Post norms visibly at every retro. Revisit quarterly.

## Anonymous Input Tools

- Slido / Mentimeter: polls + open questions
- EasyRetro anonymous mode: stickies without names
- Google Forms: pre-retro survey
- Physical: folded paper in a bowl (co-located)

## Trust-Building Activities (when safety <3)

- **Appreciation round**: each person names one thing they appreciate about someone else
- **Personal maps**: share something non-work (hobby, recent learning)
- **Working agreements review**: revisit and update team norms together
- **Facilitator vulnerability**: facilitator shares their own mistake/learning first

## Ongoing Safety Monitoring

- Run safety check every retro (takes 1min)
- Track score over time  -  trend matters more than single point
- Score drop of 1+ point: investigate (1:1s, not public)
- If score consistently <3.5: systemic issue  -  escalate to manager/HR

## Verification

- [ ] Safety check conducted (score recorded)
- [ ] Score <3 triggered trust-building (not forced retro)
- [ ] Norms posted visibly
- [ ] Anonymous option available for sensitive input
- [ ] Score trend tracked over time
