# Stakeholder Management

## Scope

Manage stakeholder relationships, communication cadence, escalation paths, and trust building. Ensure alignment between delivery teams, leadership, and customers.

## Core Principles

1. RAG status on every initiative weekly - no exceptions
2. Bad news early: surface risks before they become crises
3. Weekly update format: shipped / next / at-risk
4. Know your audience: exec=outcomes, team=details, customer=impact
5. Decisions documented with context + rationale + date
6. Escalation path clear: when, to whom, with what data
7. Regular 1:1s with key stakeholders (min biweekly)
8. No surprises rule - if Red, stakeholder heard Amber first
9. Data over opinion in every update
10. Action items from every meeting: owner + due date + follow-up

## DO NOT

1. Surprise leadership with Red status without prior Amber signal
2. Send updates without clear ask or next steps
3. Bury the recommendation in data
4. Skip stakeholder mapping on new initiatives
5. Let conflicts fester past 2 meetings without escalation

## Decision Matrix

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Milestone slipping >3d | Amber status + mitigation plan |
| Blocker unresolved 48h | Escalate with options |
| Stakeholder disengaged | 1:1 to re-align |
| New initiative starting | Stakeholder map + RACI |
| Decision needed | Decision record with recommendation |
| Cross-team dependency risk | Pre-align with owning team |

## Verification

- Every update has RAG + shipped/next/risk
- Escalations include: problem, impact, options, recommendation
- Decision records exist for all non-trivial choices
- Stakeholder map exists for active initiatives
- No Red status without prior Amber communication
- Action items tracked with owner + date

## AI-Era Context (2026)

AI drafts status updates, exec summaries, and decision records from ticket activity and PR data. It generates first drafts of RACI matrices and stakeholder maps from org charts and communication patterns. However, tone calibration, political context, and relationship management need human review. AI cannot sense when a stakeholder is losing confidence or when an update needs to be delivered in person rather than async. Use AI to draft, human to finalize and deliver.
