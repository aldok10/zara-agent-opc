# Conflict Resolution

## Scope

Resolve priority disputes, resource contention, and approach disagreements. Preserve relationships while unblocking delivery.

## Conflict Types

| Type | Signal | Approach |
|------|--------|----------|
| Priority | "My project is more important" | Align to shared goals/metrics |
| Resource | "I need that person/budget" | Data-driven allocation criteria |
| Approach | "We should do X not Y" | Prototype/evidence, decision record |
| Interpersonal | Tension in meetings, avoidance | 1:1s, find shared interest |

## Resolution Framework (Fisher/Ury)

1. **Separate people from problem** - attack the issue, not the person
2. **Focus on interests, not positions** - ask "why" behind the demand
3. **Generate options for mutual gain** - brainstorm before deciding
4. **Use objective criteria** - metrics, precedent, market data

## Process

```
1. Acknowledge: "I see we disagree on X"
2. Understand: "Help me understand what's driving your view"
3. Reframe: "We both want [shared goal]. The question is how."
4. Options: "What if we tried A, B, or C?"
5. Criteria: "Let's decide based on [metric/principle]"
6. Commit: Document decision + rationale + review date
```

## De-escalation Tactics

- Move from group to 1:1
- Restate their position accurately (shows listening)
- Find the 10% you agree with, start there
- Propose time-boxed experiment over permanent decision
- Bring data, remove emotion from the frame

## When to Escalate

- 2+ meetings without resolution
- Blocking delivery timeline
- Relationship damage affecting collaboration
- Decision above your authority level
- Parties unwilling to engage in good faith

## Escalation Format

```
CONFLICT: [one sentence description]
PARTIES: [who]
IMPACT: [what's blocked, cost of delay]
ATTEMPTED: [what you tried]
ASK: [specific decision needed from escalation target]
```

## Anti-patterns

- Avoiding the conflict (it grows)
- Triangulating (talking about, not to)
- Forcing a decision without buy-in (creates resistance)
- Relitigating settled decisions without new data

## Verification

- Both parties' interests articulated before solution proposed
- Decision documented with rationale
- Follow-up scheduled to check resolution held
- No escalation without at least one direct resolution attempt
