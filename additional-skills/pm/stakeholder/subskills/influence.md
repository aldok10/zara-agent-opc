# Influence Without Authority

## Scope

Build alignment, create coalitions, frame decisions. Get things done through others.

## Stakeholder Mapping: Power/Interest Grid

```
         HIGH POWER
    +---------+---------+
    | Keep    | Manage  |
    | Satisfied| Closely |
    +---------+---------+
    | Monitor | Keep    |
    |         | Informed|
    +---------+---------+
         LOW POWER
   LOW INTEREST   HIGH INTEREST
```

## Strategy Per Quadrant

| Quadrant | Strategy |
|----------|----------|
| High power, high interest | Collaborate closely, regular 1:1s, co-create solutions |
| High power, low interest | Keep satisfied, brief updates, escalate only when needed |
| Low power, high interest | Keep informed, leverage as advocates, gather input |
| Low power, low interest | Monitor, minimal effort, batch communications |

## Influence Tactics

1. **Pre-meeting alignment** - never surprise decision-makers in group settings
2. **Coalition building** - align supporters before the ask
3. **Loss aversion framing** - "if we don't X, we lose Y" > "if we X, we gain Y"
4. **Social proof** - "Team A already adopted this approach"
5. **Authority** - reference data, experts, precedent
6. **Reciprocity** - help them first, ask second
7. **Timing** - ask when momentum is positive, not during crisis

## Process for Big Decisions

```
1. Map stakeholders (power/interest)
2. Identify blockers and champions
3. 1:1 pre-alignment with key players
4. Address blocker concerns privately
5. Present in group with visible support
6. Document decision immediately
```

## Building Trust Capital

- Deliver on small promises consistently
- Share credit publicly, take blame privately
- Be the person who follows up
- Provide value before asking for things
- Be honest about bad news (builds credibility)

## Anti-patterns

- Going alone on decisions that affect others
- Ambushing stakeholders in meetings with new info
- Ignoring blockers (they don't disappear)
- Over-relying on one champion
- Asking without giving first
- Framing as zero-sum when it isn't

## Verification

- Stakeholder map exists before major initiative
- Pre-alignment conversations documented
- No decisions announced without prior 1:1 with affected parties
- Blockers addressed directly, not avoided
- Coalition visible in meeting (not just you advocating)
