## Comparison Resources

| Topic | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Katalon vs Playwright | Katalon: multi-platform, codeless. Playwright: faster, OSS, code-first. |
| Katalon vs Cypress | Katalon: enterprise reporting, mobile. Cypress: JS-native, fast feedback. |
| Katalon vs Selenium | Katalon: managed framework. Selenium: max flexibility, build-your-own. |
| When to choose Katalon | Mixed-skill teams, multi-platform, enterprise governance, rapid ramp-up. |
| When to avoid Katalon | All-dev teams, web-only, budget-limited, need OSS portability. |
