# Decision Records

## Core Concepts
- Decision records document choices made during a project's lifecycle beyond just architecture
- Types: technology selection, process changes, team agreements, prioritization calls
- Lightweight format: one page or less, focused on context + decision + impact
- Discovery-friendly: searchable, tagged, and indexed for future reference
- Accountability: records who made the decision, when, and with what information

## Key Practices
- Use a standard template: Date, Participants, Context, Options Considered, Decision, Follow-up Actions
- Store decision records where the team already works (wiki, repo, project management tool)
- Tag with relevant domains (performance, security, cost, UX) for discoverability
- Reference supporting evidence: benchmark results, spike findings, user research
- Review periodically: are past decisions still valid given new information?

## Common Mistakes
- Capturing only the final decision without the reasoning or alternatives considered
- Scattering decisions across meeting notes, Slack threads, and emails (unfindable)
- Decision records that require a 30-minute read (brevity enables adoption)

## When to Apply
- Team makes a non-obvious choice that future members will question
- Cross-team decisions that affect shared interfaces or processes
- Prioritization calls where tradeoffs need to be documented for stakeholders
